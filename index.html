    Using evidence like a social scientist

    STATEMENT

    Tourism can put pressure on the housing of the people who live in a destination.

    EVIDENCE (with the source named)

    In April 2024 there were 698 Airbnb listings on Waiheke Island and only 9 long-term rentals advertised on Trade Me (University of Auckland, 2024).

    EXPLANATION (the bit most people skip)

    The NZ Herald reported in 2018 that a Waiheke house renting for $600 a week in winter could earn $600 a night in summer, so some owners switch to short stays. That leaves fewer homes for the workers the island needs.

    QUALIFICATION (the top-marks move)

    Tourism is not the only cause — holiday homes and the cost of building matter too — and about one island job in six is in accommodation and food, so the same visitors also pay local wages.
